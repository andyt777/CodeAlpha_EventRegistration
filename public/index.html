FAQ

Good questions

Do I have to pay anything?
No. Creating an account and registering for events on Gatherly is free for attendees. Organizers can publish events for free too.
What if I can't make it after registering?
Open "My tickets" and click Cancel — any time before the event starts. Your seat goes back to the pool immediately and you can register again later if there's room.
The event is full. Is there anything I can do?
Seats open up whenever someone cancels, and organizers sometimes increase capacity. Check back on the event page — the seat counter updates in real time.
What do you do with my data?
We only ask for your name, email and (optionally) a phone number. Your password is stored as a salted hash. Only the organizer of an event you register for can see your details for that event.
